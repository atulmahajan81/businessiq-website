Learn Franchising

Everything an investor in India needs to understand before signing a franchise agreement — written plainly, with no sales pitch.

Home › Learn

Most people evaluating their first franchise are handed a brochure and a number, and are expected to make a decision worth tens of lakhs from that alone. The vocabulary is unfamiliar, the costs are quoted inconsistently, and the parts that matter most over ten years — royalties, renewal terms, territory definitions — are rarely the parts anyone leads with.

These pages exist to close that gap. They explain how franchising actually works in India: what you pay and when, what the agreement commits you to, how the models differ, and when franchising is the wrong choice. BusinessIQ is an Authorized Independent Franchise Consultant, so we have an interest in you investing — which is exactly why this section is written to be useful rather than persuasive. An investor who understands the model is a better franchisee than one who does not.
