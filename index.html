Frequently Asked Questions

Your Franchise Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about buying a franchise in India — investment, timelines, process, and more.

What is the minimum investment to start a franchise in India?

The minimum investment starts from ₹5 Lakhs for kiosk and micro-franchise models such as coaching or kids education franchises. Standard unit franchises typically require ₹15–50 Lakhs, while premium formats and master franchise rights range from ₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crore+. BusinessIQ has opportunities across every investment tier.

How long does it take to open a franchise in India?

Typically 3 to 6 months from initial consultation to opening day, depending on the brand selected, location finalisation, legal agreements, and outlet fit-out. Some lean formats like coaching and education franchises can launch in as little as 6–8 weeks.

What is an Independent Franchise Consultant (IFC)?

An IFC is an authorised business advisor who helps investors find, evaluate, and acquire the right franchise opportunity. BusinessIQ is an Authorised IFC operating under Franchise India Consultants Pvt. Ltd., India's largest franchise consulting network — meaning you get expert guidance backed by India's most comprehensive franchise database.

Which franchise categories are most popular in India in 2026?

The top-performing franchise categories in India in 2026 are:

  • Food & Beverage — cafés, QSRs, dessert chains
  • Education & Training — skill development, kids programs
  • Health & Wellness — gyms, wellness clinics, Pilates
  • Business Services & Coaching — consulting, training centres
  • Retail & Lifestyle — fashion, beauty, homewear

International brands from the UK, USA, and Australia are in particularly high demand.

Is franchising a good investment in India?

Yes. Franchising offers significantly lower risk than starting from scratch because you get a proven business model, established brand recognition, training, and ongoing operational support. India's franchise industry is growing at 30–35% annually — one of the fastest-growing franchise markets globally — with strong consumer demand across Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities.

What cities in India have the best franchise opportunities?

Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata are India's top franchise markets. However, Tier-2 cities like Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Surat, and Lucknow are fast-growing opportunities with lower real estate costs and rising consumer spending — ideal for first-time franchise investors.

Do I need prior business experience to buy a franchise?

Not necessarily. Most international franchise brands provide comprehensive training before launch and ongoing support afterwards. BusinessIQ's advisory process helps match you with brands that suit your background, skills, and the level of hands-on involvement you prefer — whether you're a first-time entrepreneur or an experienced business owner.
