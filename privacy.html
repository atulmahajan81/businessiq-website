Last updated: 26 June 2026

BusinessIQ.co.in ("BusinessIQ", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website www.businessiq.co.in or interact with our services.

Please read this policy carefully. By using our website, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and city provided via our enquiry or contact forms.
  • Business Information: Preferred franchise category, investment budget, and business goals shared voluntarily.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs — collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communication Records: Records of emails, WhatsApp messages, or calls when you contact us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your franchise enquiries and connect you with relevant opportunities
  • Provide personalised franchise advisory and consultation services

  • Improve our website content and user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only:

  • With Franchise India Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (FICPL) — our principal organisation — as BusinessIQ operates as an Authorized IFC of FICPL. All enquiry data is routed to FICPL to process your franchise consultation request.

  • When required by law, court order, or governmental authority

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable laws. Enquiry data is typically retained for up to 3 years.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@businessiq.co.in.
